About Nomádico

More than a car rental company

We are Marco and Laurien, a French-Belgian couple with a deep love for Mexico and Central America. Our story began when we met while travelling through Nicaragua, and just a few months later, we found ourselves exploring Mexico together. We dreamed of discovering the country by campervan, but quickly realized there were none available for rent—only a few for sale. So, we took a leap and bought our first home on wheels. With our old Dodge Ram, Pancho, we travelled through southern Mexico. When COVID hit, we continued our van-life adventures back in Europe, with Mexico always on our minds. Now, a few years later, we’re excited to make van-life accessible to everyone!
